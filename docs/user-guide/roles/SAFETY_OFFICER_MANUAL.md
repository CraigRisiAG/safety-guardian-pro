# Safety Guardian Pro Safety Officer Manual

## Purpose

This manual is for safety officers running operational incident and drill workflows.

## Access level

Safety officers typically use:

- Dashboard
- Incidents
- Drills
- Safety Check-In

## Core responsibilities

- Report and update incidents.
- Start, schedule, and complete drills.
- Drive check-in completion during active drills.
- Review drill outcomes and accountability statistics.

## Daily workflow

1. Open Dashboard and check Open Incidents and Scheduled Drills.
2. Update incident statuses to reflect current handling.
3. Prepare drill schedules and confirm scope (building/floor/area).
4. During active drills, monitor check-in progress and support unresolved statuses.

## Incident handling process

1. Open Incidents.
2. Create a new incident with severity and location.
3. Assign/track follow-up actions.
4. Progress status from Open to In Progress to Closed.
5. Use trend charts for period reporting.

## Drill operation process

1. Open Drills.
2. Start Drill for immediate activation or Schedule Drill for later.
3. Monitor active status in Dashboard and Drill views.
4. Open Safety Check-In to track personnel states.
5. End drill when accountability process is complete.
6. Export drill history for reporting.

## Common issues and fixes

- Safety Check-In unavailable: verify drill is active.
- Personnel not found: confirm user exists in personnel records and mapped location.
- Drill stats look incomplete: ensure drill was ended to finalize history records.
